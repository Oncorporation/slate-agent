# Production assets

From film-production-skills `design-production-assets`.

Inventory characters, locations, props. Stable names. Identity anchors (must match every shot) vs allowed variation (wet, night, damaged).

Locations: geography, lighting baseline, scale, entry/exit. Story props get a row.

Write/upsert `assets.md`. Specifications, not images.
