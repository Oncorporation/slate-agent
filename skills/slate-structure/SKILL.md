# Structure

Feature ~110 pages: Act 1 1–25, Act 2 25–85, Act 3 85–110. Shorts and concepts scale the same ratios.

Save the Cat: Opening Image, Theme Stated, Setup, Catalyst, Debate, Break into Two, B Story, Fun & Games, Midpoint, Bad Guys Close In, All Is Lost, Dark Night, Break into Three, Finale, Final Image (opposite of opening).

Rhythm from observable change: objective, action, relationship, information, emotion, or space.

Write `beats.md` from `templates/beat-sheet.md`. Do not write dialogue here.
